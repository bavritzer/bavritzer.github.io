About Me

I am a Quantum Scientist at Leidos, where I work primarily on quantum communications and networking projects, including QSDC, error correction, and quantum sensing. Prior to this employment, I completed my Ph.D. in physics at the University of Southern California (where I maintain an affiliation), under the supervision of Dr. Todd Brun. My doctoral work focused on communication methods, especially steganographic methods, and how they can be used for covert communication of quantum information. I also partake in various hobbies, some of which you may find listed on this webpage.
